### Latest result

**Do plant DNA language models beat k-mers at predicting gene expression?** Yes, by a wide margin. Frozen embeddings
from AgroNT (1B parameters, 48 plant genomes) predicted expression across 56 *Arabidopsis* tissues and conditions from
6 kb sequences with mean test Spearman ρ = 0.58, vs. 0.34 for the best k-mer model on the same 5,000 genes, and beat
k-mers trained on 5× more data. → [plant-dnalm-expression](https://github.com/Olaraym/plant-dnalm-expression)
